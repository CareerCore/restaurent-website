Our Story

Celebrating 30 Years of Authentic Flavors

Tandoori was first established in 1993 in Islamabad. We started with zeal and still serve with the same enthusiasm. Our devotion makes our vision broader and our hard work makes our food legendary.

From our humble beginnings at G-8 Markaz to multiple branches across Islamabad, we've been serving thousands of families with clean environment, top-quality ingredients, and unforgettable taste.
